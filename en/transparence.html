How to read this table

Three possible states, never a blank. not_offered: no offer exists in the catalog — different from "€0", confusing the two would already be a small dishonesty. gray: measurable in principle, nothing to measure today, with the reason written out in plain language. A third state, measured, appears the day a real order (not sandbox) exists for a service — none is today.

The founder's time is excluded from this calculation, explicitly — no measurement source exists for it yet, and omitting it silently would have been simpler but less honest.

The ledger itself (source code) traces every figure back down to a supporting document — no displayed amount can exist without a verifiable derivation path.
