Asymmetry as a human reference system

There is today no shared, irrefutable, non-normative human visual referent that would let one observe a human being's state without interpreting, classifying or judging it. Existing systems classify, score, predict, normalize. Aiame observes.

Asymmetry is not an anomaly,
it is the minimal signature of the living.

B2H — the essential distinction

Neither dopamine-oriented B2C, nor cold-performance-oriented B2B. B2H makes the human the ultimate beneficiary — the institution is a support, never a goal. It is a relationship of reciprocal trust where the product never tries to change the user, coded as a structural constraint in AIAME's foundation.

Reversed dependencyConsult, ignore or leave — at no cost. No artificial retention.
Silent by defaultStructural indicators, never conclusions. The indicator stays mute as long as individual benefit isn't proven.
Value without extractionNo other human is a reference. No ML base is normative. No data monetized.

The foundation is formal: symmetry is not given, it is computed — and that computation is only possible starting from asymmetry.

Research that sells nothing

This research is deliberately kept separate from AIAME's commercial activity (aiame.fr). It is protected by its own rules: a validation protocol with explicit abandonment criteria, an indicator deliberately kept mute as long as the benefit for the individual isn't scientifically established, and an internal red team whose role is to break the framework before anyone else does.
