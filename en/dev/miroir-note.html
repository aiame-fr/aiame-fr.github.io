An instrument, not an oracle

Aiame Miroir measures the geometry of one person's face and hand in a single frame of reference, in the browser, without sending anything anywhere. It renders a measured reading — zone, signal, type, confidence — never an interpretation.

Same data, two possible readings.
Here, only the measured one is enabled by default.

What it does

The face and hand share the same geometric computation: symmetry plane, gaps, lengths, angles — normalized, comparable over time, never compared to anyone else. The hand is not a minor extension of the face: it measures its own asymmetry, independently, and can nuance or contradict it — that is intentional.

MeasuredWhat the instrument displays by default: values, a confidence, never a judgment.
LocalCamera capture, computation, display — everything happens in your browser. Nothing is stored, nothing is sent.
