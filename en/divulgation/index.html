Disclosure rule · 2026-10-08

What this showcase shows, and what it withholds.

We hold to proof and honesty. We do not hold to handing anyone who might take advantage the list of what is not yet fixed. This page writes down where the line runs, so that the restraint itself stays verifiable.

The rule

1. A finding is published once fixed — with its date and its evidence, so that anyone can re-check it.

2. While it is open, it appears only as a number. The count is the detail’s count: the text is removed, never the figure. A page showing “0 absent” because it hid the absent ones would be lying.

3. Internal topology is never published. Machine names, private network, administration paths: replaced by generic names, and publication fails if one survives.

4. What is stale is removed. A snapshot that goes stale is not left in place “for transparency”: it misleads.

Where it shows

On the galaxy, each repository shows what is proven, declared and written ABSENT, with the counts. The name and evidence of an absent capability, and the list of what remains open, are not published while open: they are counted.

The public page is generated, never written by hand. Its digest is in galaxie/PROVENANCE.json. The full detail lives in a private repository; each guard of the public projection was seen to fail on a mutation before being trusted.

The portfolio left the showcase

Until 2026-10-08, the home page was a “portfolio”: a dated audit of 37 repositories that wrote down their open defects. The owner took it off: it duplicated the galaxy and named findings that were still open. The galaxy is now the home page.

Evidence. The former home page is archived, byte for byte, in a private repository. Its sum can be recomputed by anyone from the site repository’s public history: git show 34940c5:index.html | sha256sum → e5702cf482098b610897f0cfbbf484f45517ba05e52539b96dd83ff3e338b785 (English version : git show 34940c5:en/index.html | sha256sum → b3599189a79d4715983e46d5200b8dafa5b8276261a8545e06a2b358822f28b1).

What this does not prove. That these pages were never read: they were served publicly, and the site repository is public with its whole history. Removing recalls nothing. We do not rewrite that history without a dated owner decision, and we do not claim that third-party copies or archives do not exist.

The old addresses now only redirect to the home page (/portfolio/, /en/, the August snapshot).
