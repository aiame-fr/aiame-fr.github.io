Where Aiame Red stands

Aiame Red is an instrument, not an offer. So far it has run only on our own products and documents. It has never been run on a third party's product: no outside proof exists yet.

  • Nothing has been sold. This page offers no order, no payment, no price and no delivery time.
  • The public ledger classes the service as "offered_sandbox_only", revenue shown in grey: see the financial transparency page.
  • Known limit: the engine checks the presence of what a description declares — a stop condition, retention, consent — not the quality of what is declared.

An offer would only be justified after a run on a third party's described capability, with their agreement. That run has not happened.

B2H — Business to Human

B2C dopamine-oriented. B2B cold-performance-oriented. B2H: the human as ultimate beneficiary — the institution as a support, never as the goal.

A reciprocal, win-win relationship of trust between a product and its user: the product never tries to change the user. This is AIAME's formalized framework — coded as a structural constraint, not marketing talk — and it is the benchmark of every Aiame Red audit: the engine measures a product's description against it.

Reversed dependencyThe user can leave at no cost. No streaks, no addictive notifications, no artificial retention.
Silent by defaultThe product speaks only on request. No judgment, no prescription, no interpretation of the person.
Value without extractionNo data monetization. Anonymous aggregates, explicit opt-in, never correlatable to the individual.

If the user feels they must progress, compare, perform — B2H is dead. It's a test, not a slogan.

What the engine is designed to produce

None of the following has been handed to a third party.

  • Drift sheets per capability: plausible abusive readings, realistic malicious actor, semantic-drift test, GDPR & dignity test.
  • Misuse simulation played against the description of a product — intent, exposed labels, data, outputs, autonomy — not against the running product: every finding is deterministic, reproducible and traced to the exact field that produced it. No slides.
  • AI Act mapping (art. 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 26, 50) and GDPR (art. 5, 22, 25, 35): evidence usable by a DPO, counsel or an auditor.
  • Stop conditions: the engine checks that they exist — who decides, within what time, with what test.

Our method comes from our own internal rule: every visible capability has its adversarial counter-reading before any public release. A good Red Team result is: "here is exactly where this system no longer knows what it's doing."

The engine runs on us first

Our internal rule requires that every visible capability have its adversarial counter-reading before any public release. We couldn't hold ourselves to it by hand: so the engine was built for our own needs, and it runs on us once a week (Mondays, 06:17 UTC) — a scheduled pass, not a trigger on every deployment. It re-reads the description of each of our tracked products and flags any drift from a versioned reference state (for instance a missing stop condition). It also checks, read-only, that two live services respond as expected: the copy of this site published on aiame-fr.github.io (HTTP status codes only, not content) and our sov.services.aiame.fr service.

It is the tool we hold ourselves to. Our own sheets are not published at this time.

What we will never do

  • Audit people (employees, candidates, customers) — we audit systems.
  • Produce a scoring of individuals, even "for guidance", even "to help".
  • Sell, retain or reuse your data or your users' data.
  • A complacent report. House rule: if we don't know, we write "we don't know".

The engine produces technical findings, not legal advice. The AI Act / GDPR mapping documents evidence usable by a DPO, counsel or an auditor; it is neither legal advice nor a certification of compliance.
